How to use OmniVoyager

  1. Tap 🤖 Prompt, describe the machine or mechanism you want to see, and tap "Create a new model". A request for the AI is copied.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc. and send it. When the answer is complete, copy the whole answer.
  3. Come back and tap 🔁 Apply AI. The 3D model appears (long-press to open the manual paste screen).
  4. Drag with one finger to rotate, use two fingers to zoom and pan, and double-tap to reset the view. Tap a part to see its explanation.
  5. Use the buttons on the right to toggle labels, section, X-ray, motion and speed. Tap 🎬 to play the guided tour.
  6. To change something, use 🤖 Prompt again. A fix patch changes only the parts that need it. To raise the quality, use 🧐 Consult.
  7. Tap 💾 Save to store it in the library. Every version is kept and can be opened any time.
  8. With ⋯ More → 🎵 Import BGM, you can use a SonicForge song (ABC notation) as BGM. Turn it on or off with "BGM" on the right. It also plays in exported HTML.

The 3D engine (Three.js) is loaded from the internet, so models can't be shown offline. Your model code is saved in this device's browser.
